# TMAegis™ Trademark Policy

*Last updated: October 2026*

The TMAegis source code is open source under the [Mozilla Public License 2.0](LICENSE).
The **brand** is not. This policy explains what you can and cannot do with the name and
the logo, so that the community can use TMAegis freely while users can always tell the
official project from a fork or a product built on it.

## 1. Our marks

The following are unregistered or pending trademarks, jointly owned by the project
founders, **Tommy Huynh** (Vietnam) and **Misha Milanovic** (Australia)
(together, the "Founders"):

- **TMAegis™** and **tmaegis** (the name, including as a command, binary, package, or product name)
- the **TM Cyber-Shield logo**: `assets/logo.svg` and `assets/logo-mark.svg`, and
  derivatives that look confusingly similar

Section 2.3 of the MPL-2.0 states that it grants no trademark rights. Nothing in the
`LICENSE`, in the source code, or in a contribution grants you permission to use the marks
other than as set out below.

## 2. What you may do (nominative and fair use)

You do **not** need permission to:

- write tutorials, blog posts, articles, videos, courses, or talks about TMAegis;
- state truthfully that your tool, rule pack, plugin, or integration **works with** or is
  **compatible with** TMAegis (for example, "Acme Plugin for TMAegis");
- run the **unmodified** official binaries or the unmodified CLI inside your company or
  for your clients' own systems, and mention that you used TMAegis in an audit report;
- link to the official repository and describe the project accurately;
- refer to TMAegis by name in a benchmark comparison, security review, or academic work;
- use the name in plain text that makes clear who the owner is, for example:
  "TMAegis™ is a trademark of its Founders."

Keep it truthful: do not suggest endorsement, partnership, or official certification status
that does not exist, and use only as much of the brand as necessary to identify the software.

## 3. What you may not do

Without a written commercial licence from the Founders you may **not**:

1. **Redistribute commercially under the TMAegis name**, whether the original or a
   modified build, as a paid product, appliance, bundle, or managed service.
2. **Sell or offer managed SaaS, hosted, or cloud-marketplace images** (AWS, Azure, GCP,
   DigitalOcean, container registries, or VM marketplaces) **using the TMAegis brand**.
3. **Use the TM Cyber-Shield logo on forked or modified binaries**, installers,
   packages, containers, websites, or documentation.
4. **Name a fork, product, company, domain, social account, app-store listing,
   package, or repository** in a way that is the same as or confusingly similar to
   TMAegis (including `tmaegis-pro`, `tmaegis-enterprise`, `TM-Aegis`, `TMAgeis`, `TMAgies`, ...).
5. Imply that your product, rule pack, or service is official, certified, or endorsed by the
   Founders or the TMAegis project.
6. Register the marks, confusingly similar marks, or domain names containing them in any jurisdiction.
7. Use the marks in a way that is misleading, disparaging, or associated with unlawful activity.

## 4. Forks and modified versions: rebrand requirement

You are free to fork the source code under the MPL-2.0. However, if you distribute a modified
version **commercially**, you must:

- **remove the TMAegis name and the TM Cyber-Shield logo** from the product (binary
  name, CLI banner, `--help`, desktop GUI, reports, installers, package metadata, website, and
  documentation), and replace them with your own distinct name and branding;
- retain all licence notices required by the MPL-2.0 (the `LICENSE` file, copyright and
  SPDX headers, and the `NOTICE` file), and state clearly in your documentation that your
  product is **derived from the open-source TMAegis project**, for example:
  *"Based on the open-source TMAegis project."*

A non-commercial community fork that is clearly labelled as unofficial may retain
references to the original name solely in the nominative manner described in Section 2.

## 5. Commercial licence and permission

If you wish to use the marks in a way not covered by this policy (for example, an
official partner programme, OEM embedding, white-label/co-branded reports, or a cloud marketplace listing),
please contact the Founders at `contact@tmaegis.dev`, by opening an issue in the official repository, or via the
contact details in [`AUTHORS`](AUTHORS). Permission must be granted **in writing**.

## 6. Enforcement

We may request that you cease any use not permitted by this policy and transfer or
cancel infringing names, domains, or marketplace listings. Our goal is always to be constructive:
tell us what you are building and we will usually find a straightforward path that works for everyone.
